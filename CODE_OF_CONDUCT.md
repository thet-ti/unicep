# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes being welcoming and respectful, accepting constructive feedback
gracefully, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers through GitHub, by contacting
them privately via their profile.
